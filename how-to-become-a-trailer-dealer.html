How to become a trailer dealer

Start with your customers, a suitable lot, and a plan for selling and supporting the trailers you carry. Here are the steps to discuss with a manufacturer before an opening order.

By Triple R Trailers · Updated September 9, 2026

Start with a business and customer plan

Identify the customers you serve, the loads they haul, and the space you can dedicate to displaying trailers. Review your budget for inventory, delivery, equipment, and ongoing operations before selecting a product line.

Check the requirements for your location

Confirm applicable business and dealer requirements with the relevant local and state offices. Discuss display space, unloading arrangements, and service capability with your manufacturer. A manufacturer application is separate from any licensing or registration your business needs.

Compare manufacturers and dealer terms

Ask for itemized quotes, current ordering and delivery arrangements, warranty documents, and the territory terms that apply to your address. Use our manufacturer selection checklist to prepare for that conversation.

Choose an opening product mix

Use customer requests and your sales experience to choose the trailer categories you want to stock. Compare costs and capacity before committing to an order. Ask what is stocked, what must be built, and how the factory communicates changes.

Apply and review the next steps

  1. Send your business details and proposed location through the dealer application.
  2. Discuss location availability, current requirements, and written dealer terms with the office.
  3. If approved, confirm the opening order, delivery arrangements, and dealer portal access.

Submitting an application does not reserve a territory or guarantee approval. Call (662) 728-7975 to talk through your plans with Triple R.
