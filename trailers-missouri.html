Trailers for sale in Missouri that outlast the odometer

Missouri buyers put miles on trailers: hauling equipment across the Bootheel, moving cars up I-55, working cattle and crops in between. Our 82x20 car hauler, 7x14 dump, and tandem utility trailers are built for exactly that duty cycle, welded from heavy-duty steel in Booneville, Mississippi.

Buyers in Sikeston, Cape Girardeau, Poplar Bluff, Kennett, and Dexter reach our line through dealers in the region, with factory parts and service a day trip away instead of a shipping ticket.

Trailers Missouri buyers ask us for

  • Utility trailers from 5x8 to 7x16 tandem
  • Enclosed cargo trailers from 5x8 to 8.5x24, with the Black Out package
  • Dump trailers: 7x14 tandem with a 14,000 lb GVWR
  • Car haulers with an 82x20 steel deck
  • Equipment trailers and made to order goosenecks

Where can I buy a Triple R trailer in Missouri?
