Why go gooseneck

A gooseneck trailer puts the hitch weight over your truck's rear axle instead of hanging it off the bumper. That means more capacity, tighter turning, and a far more stable pull when the load gets long or heavy. It is the setup serious haulers graduate to, and it is the one line we build entirely to order.

Because every gooseneck leaves Booneville specced to a real customer, you are not buying a compromise off a lot. Deck length, dovetail, ramps, axle count and rating, tie-downs, toolboxes: it all comes off our options list and gets welded into heavy-duty steel by the same crew that has built over 50,000 trailers since 1997.

FAQ

Gooseneck questions

Does Triple R build gooseneck trailers?

Yes. Gooseneck trailers are our made to order line. Every build is specced to your load and truck, welded from heavy-duty steel in Booneville, Mississippi.

How do I order a gooseneck trailer?

Start with your nearest Triple R dealer or call the factory at (662) 728-7975. Tell us what you haul and we will spec the frame, deck, and axle package to match.

How long does a made to order gooseneck take?

Lead times change with the season and the build. Call the factory for the current schedule before you plan your first haul.

Why choose a gooseneck over a bumper pull trailer?

A gooseneck moves the hitch weight over your truck's rear axle, which allows more capacity, better stability, and tighter turning than a bumper pull at the same length.
