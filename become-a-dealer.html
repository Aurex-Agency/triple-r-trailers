FAQ

Dealer program questions

What does it take to become a Triple R trailer dealer?

Fill out the dealer application and be located at least 60 miles from our nearest existing dealer. That is it. No inflated first order requirements, no games.

What is the 60 mile protected territory?

Every Triple R dealer is protected by a 60 mile radius: we will not sign another dealer within 60 miles of your lot. Your market stays your market.

What support do dealers get from Triple R?

Dealer pricing, delivery, marketing support, and factory backup on parts and service. When you carry our line, the factory is on your side of the counter.

How do dealers see pricing?

Approved dealers get a login to the dealer portal where current price lists and the full options list are always available. No waiting on a faxed sheet.
