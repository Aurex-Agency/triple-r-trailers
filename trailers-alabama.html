Trailers for sale in Alabama without the freight markup

North Alabama is Triple R country. The Shoals, Huntsville, Decatur, Athens, and Cullman are a short haul from our Booneville factory, and Alabama dealers keep our utility, enclosed, and dump lines stocked for local buyers.

Alabama work is hard on trailers: red clay, timber, row crops, and construction sites do not forgive light gauge steel. Every Triple R that crosses the state line was welded from heavy-duty steel with no shortcuts, and every one is backed by a factory that stocks its own parts.

Trailers Alabama buyers ask us for

  • Utility trailers from 5x8 to 7x16 tandem
  • Enclosed cargo trailers from 5x8 to 8.5x24, with the Black Out package
  • Dump trailers: 7x14 tandem with a 14,000 lb GVWR
  • Car haulers with an 82x20 steel deck
  • Equipment trailers and made to order goosenecks

Why Alabama buys Booneville steel

  • Factory direct pricing through your local dealer
  • Heavy-duty steel on every build, no light gauge
  • 2 year enclosed and 1 year utility warranties, in writing
  • Factory parts and service one call away

Alabama trailer questions

Where can I buy a Triple R trailer in Alabama?

Triple R dealers serve Alabama, especially the northern half of the state around Huntsville and the Shoals. Call (662) 728-7975 and we will route you to the closest lot.

How far is the Triple R factory from Alabama?

The Booneville, Mississippi factory sits about 30 minutes from the Alabama state line, an easy drive for pickup, parts, or service.

What trailers do Alabama buyers choose most?

Dump trailers for land and construction work, utility trailers for farms, and enclosed cargo trailers for trades. All are built with heavy-duty steel and written warranties.
