FAQ

Trailer buying questions

What types of trailers does Triple R build?

We build utility trailers, enclosed cargo trailers, dump trailers, car haulers, equipment trailers, and made to order gooseneck trailers, all from heavy-duty steel at our factory in Booneville, Mississippi.

How much does a Triple R trailer cost?

Pricing runs through our dealer network, which keeps it competitive with any lot in the Mid-South. Call your nearest dealer for a price on the lot, or request a quote from the factory and we will price the exact build you need.

Where can I buy a Triple R trailer?

Through any of our 57 dealers across Mississippi, Tennessee, Alabama, Arkansas, Missouri, and Louisiana. Use the dealer map on our Find a Dealer page or call the factory at (662) 728-7975.

Can I order a custom trailer?

Yes. We build custom and made to order from a full factory options list, from concession trailers to special axle setups. Start with your dealer or call the factory.

Do Triple R trailers come with a warranty?

Yes, in writing: 2 years on enclosed trailers and 1 year on utility trailers, backed by the factory that built them.
