Haul cars without holding your breath

A car hauler earns trust one load at a time. The 82 inch deck width takes everything from a mud truck to a vintage coupe, and 20 feet of length means you are not inching a bumper over the edge to close a gate. Ramps come standard, so loading is a drive-on job, not an engineering project.

Like every trailer we build, the car hauler is welded from heavy-duty steel in our Booneville shop. The deck stays flat, the coupler stays tight, and the trailer tracks straight at highway speed with a car on its back. That is what 29 years of building trailers looks like under your project car.

FAQ

Straight answers

What size car hauler does Triple R build?

Our standard car hauler is 82 inches wide by 20 feet long on two 3,500 lb axles, with ramps included for drive-on loading.

Will the 82x20 car hauler fit a full-size truck?

The 82 inch deck width and 20 foot length handle most cars, trucks, and SUVs. For heavier equipment, look at our 7x34+2 equipment trailer with 7,000 lb brake axles.

Do Triple R car haulers come with ramps?

Yes, ramps come standard on the 82x20 car hauler so you can load a vehicle without extra gear.

Where can I buy a Triple R car hauler?

Through any of our 57 dealers across the Mid-South. Use the Find a Dealer page or call the Booneville factory at (662) 728-7975.
