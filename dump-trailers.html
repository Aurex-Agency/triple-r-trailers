A dump trailer is a tool. Buy a real one.

A dump trailer takes more abuse than anything else you can hitch to a truck. Gravel slams the bed, skid steers climb the ramps, and the hoist cycles all day. That is why our 7x14 dump trailer is welded from heavy-duty steel with no light gauge shortcuts, riding on two 7,000 lb axles with brakes.

The spreader gate meters out gravel evenly instead of dumping it in a pile. The roll tarp keeps your load legal on the highway. The ramps mean your mini excavator or skid steer loads itself. It is the dump trailer setup contractors, farmers, and land owners across Mississippi and Tennessee keep coming back for.

What a 14,000 lb GVWR gets you

  • Roughly 5 tons of payload capacity for dirt, rock, and demo debris
  • Room for a compact tractor or skid steer with attachments
  • Cleanup jobs done in one trip instead of three

Standard on the 7x14 dump

  • Two 7,000 lb brake axles
  • 14,000 lb GVWR
  • Roll tarp kit
  • Loading ramps
  • Spreader gate
  • Heavy-duty steel body and frame

FAQ

Straight answers

What size dump trailer does Triple R build?

Our standard dump trailer is a 7x14 with two 7,000 lb axles and a 14,000 lb GVWR. Custom dump configurations are available made to order from the factory.

What comes standard on a Triple R dump trailer?

Every 7x14 dump trailer includes a roll tarp, loading ramps, and a spreader gate, riding on tandem 7,000 lb brake axles.

How much weight can the 7x14 dump trailer carry?

With a 14,000 lb GVWR on two 7,000 lb axles, the 7x14 handles roughly 5 tons of payload depending on options, enough for serious gravel, dirt, and equipment work.

Where can I buy a Triple R dump trailer near me?

Triple R dump trailers are sold through dealers across Mississippi, Tennessee, Alabama, Arkansas, Missouri, and Louisiana. Check the Find a Dealer page or call the factory at (662) 728-7975.
