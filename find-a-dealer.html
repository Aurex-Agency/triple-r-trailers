    FAQ

    Dealer questions

    How do I find the Triple R dealer closest to me?

    Use the dealer map above: search your city or zip, or tap Use My Location, and the nearest pinned dealers sort to the top. Or call the factory at (662) 728-7975 and we will point you to the nearest lot in under a minute.

    What if there is no Triple R dealer near me?

    No dead ends here. Call the factory or send the contact form and we will either connect you with the closest dealer that delivers or handle your quote directly from Booneville.

    Do dealers carry every trailer Triple R builds?

    Stock varies by lot. Dealers regularly carry our utility, enclosed cargo, dump, and car hauler lines, and any dealer can order any build, including custom and gooseneck orders, straight from the factory.

    Can I buy directly from the factory?

    Triple R sells through dealers. That keeps a lot near you stocked, serviced, and accountable. If you are outside our dealer footprint, call us and we will make sure you still get a trailer.
