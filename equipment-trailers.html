The long deck that hauls the heavy stuff

Equipment hauling is where cheap trailers die. Machine weight concentrates on ramps, dovetails, and crossmembers, and light gauge steel gives up fast. Our 7x34+2 equipment trailer is welded from heavy-duty steel with a 2 foot dovetail, so your equipment climbs on at a working angle and the frame shrugs off the load cycle, trip after trip.

Two 7,000 lb brake axles bring the stopping power that a loaded equipment trailer legally and practically needs. Thirty four feet of deck plus the dovetail swallows a tractor and implement together, a pair of skid steers, or a whole job site worth of material in one pull.

FAQ

Straight answers

What size equipment trailer does Triple R build?

Our standard equipment trailer is a 7x34+2, a 34 foot deck with a 2 foot dovetail, riding on two 7,000 lb brake axles. Custom equipment builds are available made to order.

Can the 7x34+2 haul a skid steer or tractor?

Yes. The 7,000 lb brake axles and heavy-duty steel frame are built for skid steers, compact tractors, mini excavators, and similar machinery.

Do Triple R equipment trailers have brakes?

Yes. Both 7,000 lb axles on the equipment trailer are brake axles, which loaded equipment hauling requires.

Can I order a different deck length?

Yes. We build custom and made to order, so talk to your dealer or call the factory about the deck length and axle setup your equipment needs.
