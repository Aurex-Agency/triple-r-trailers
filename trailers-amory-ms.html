FAQ

Amory trailer questions

Is there a Triple R trailer dealer near Amory?

Yes. The closest Triple R dealers to Amory include Chickasaw Equipment Co. in Tupelo, MS (about 25 miles), David's Used Cars in Houlka, MS (about 35 miles). Use the dealer map on our Find a Dealer page or call the factory at (662) 728-7975 and we will route you.

How far is Amory from the Triple R factory?

Roughly 55 road miles, about an hour of driving to 82 County Road 1111 in Booneville, Mississippi. Plenty of Amory buyers make the trip to see the yard, spec a build, and tow their trailer home the same day.

Can I get a trailer delivered to Amory?

Yes. Triple R trailers reach Amory through our dealer network and factory delivery, and we ship nationwide. Call (662) 728-7975 with the build you want and we will quote it delivered.
