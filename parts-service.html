What We Stock

If it goes on a trailer, we have it

Running gear

Axles, hubs, brakes, springs, and complete suspension hardware for every load rating we build.

Loading and access

Ramps, mesh gates, spreader gates, dovetail hardware, and tie-down points.

Hitching

Couplers, jacks, safety chains, and gooseneck hardware, sized to the trailer.

Electrical

LED lights, wiring harnesses, plugs, and brake controllers that pass inspection the first time.

Body and deck

FAQ

Parts and service questions

Does Triple R sell trailer parts?

Yes. We stock any part for a trailer: axles, ramps, gates, fenders, lights, jacks, couplers, wiring, and more. If it bolts or welds to a trailer, call us.

Do you service trailers you did not build?

Our shop services Triple R trailers for both dealers and the public. Call us about your trailer and we will tell you straight what we can do for it.

How do I get parts if I bought my trailer from a dealer?

Either way works: call the dealer you bought from and they will get parts from us, or come straight to the factory in Booneville and pick up what you need.

What are your shop hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 3:30, at 82 County Road 1111, Booneville, MS 38829. Call (662) 728-7975 before a long drive and we will make sure your part is on the shelf.
