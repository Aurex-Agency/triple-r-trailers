A utility trailer built like it costs more

Plenty of lots sell utility trailers that look fine on day one and sag by year two. Ours are welded in Booneville, Mississippi from heavy-duty steel, with no light gauge tubing anywhere on the trailer, even on our econo series. That is why over 50,000 Triple R trailers are still on the road working.

Every utility trailer for sale under the Triple R name comes through our own twelve-building shop and gets a treated wood deck, a full wiring harness with LED lighting, and a coat of paint that stands up to the weather. Add a mesh gate for drive-on loading, a dovetail for lawn equipment, or side racks for hauling more, and you have a trailer that fits your work instead of the other way around.

Common jobs for a Triple R utility trailer

  • Hauling zero-turn mowers and lawn care equipment
  • ATV, UTV, and golf cart transport
  • Farm and homestead chores, feed, fencing, and firewood
  • Moving appliances, furniture, and weekend projects

Standard on every utility

  • Heavy-duty steel frame, no light gauge
  • Treated wood deck
  • 3,500 lb axles, single or tandem
  • Gate and dovetail options
  • 1 year written warranty

FAQ

Straight answers

What sizes of utility trailers does Triple R build?

We build 5x8, 6x10+2, and 6.5x10+2 single axle utility trailers on 3,500 lb axles, and a 7x16 tandem utility trailer on two 3,500 lb axles. Custom sizes are available as made to order builds.

Do Triple R utility trailers come with a warranty?

Yes. Every Triple R utility trailer carries a 1 year written warranty, backed by the factory in Booneville, Mississippi.

Can I get a gate or ramp on my utility trailer?

Yes. Mesh gates and dovetail configurations are available on our utility line so you can drive equipment straight onto the deck.

Where can I buy a Triple R utility trailer?

Triple R trailers are sold through a network of 57 dealers across Mississippi, Tennessee, Alabama, Arkansas, Missouri, and Louisiana. Use our Find a Dealer page or call the factory at (662) 728-7975.
