Triple R Trailers

Booneville built, since 1997

We have been building trailers in Booneville, Mississippi since 1997. Over 50,000 trailers out the gate, twelve buildings on the lot, and one rule that has never changed: heavy-duty steel, no cut corners.

The yard on County Road 1111

Drive out to 82 County Road 1111 in Booneville and you will see what a real trailer operation looks like: twelve buildings turning out utility, enclosed cargo, dump, car hauler, equipment, and gooseneck trailers, with fresh stock staged in the yard waiting on dealer trucks.

Triple R has been building here since 1997. Ownership has changed hands along the way, but the yard, the crew's standard, and the way the trailers are built have not. New energy on the ownership side, the same heavy-duty steel on every frame.

No light gauge. Not even on the econo series.

Every manufacturer says quality. Here is what we mean by it: no light gauge steel anywhere on a Triple R trailer. Not on the flagship builds, and not on the econo series either. A budget trailer from us is a simpler trailer, never a weaker one.

That is the build philosophy that has put more than 50,000 trailers on the road, and it is why 57 dealers across six states stake their own reputations on our line.

Our warranty, in writing

Enclosed trailers carry a 2 year warranty. Utility trailers carry a 1 year warranty. Published, plain, and backed by a factory that stocks every part and answers its own phone.
